1060G42 CLUTCH PEDAL SWITCH - R R

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Disconnect the electrical connection for the clutch pedal switch.

Description

Connector

.

Clutch pedal switch

I031

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the clutch pedal switch (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the switch on the clutch pedal.

- Tighten the fixing nut until the upper and lower washers are in contact with the bracket.

- Cconnect the electrical connection for the clutch pedal switch.

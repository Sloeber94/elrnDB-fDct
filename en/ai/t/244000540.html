Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: GPL

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery terminal (-).

Disconnect the solenoid electrical connection and check the coil winding resistance is between 19 and 21 Ohm at 20 °C.

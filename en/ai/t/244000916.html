Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the solenoid valve.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

- Disconnect the rapid connector for the active charcoal filter.

- Remove the band and move the pipe away from the throttle casing.

1. Release the solenoid valve from the housing and the connected pipes.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

- Undo the bands and remove the pipes.

1. Extract the solenoid valve from the plastic housing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the solenoid valve in the plastic housing.

- Fit the pipes and fasten them using the retaining band.

- Fit the solenoid valve in the housing and the connected pipes.

- Fit the band and the pipe to the throttle casing.

- Connect the rapid connector for the active charcoal filter.

- Connect the electrical connection for the solenoid valve.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

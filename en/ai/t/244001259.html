Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Prepare a suitable container for collecting the engine coolant.

1. Disconnect the independent heater water supply (1a) and return (1b) pipes in the engine compartment removing the retaining bands.

Mark one of the two pipes to prevent them being incorrectly refitted.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Raise the vehicle on the lift.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Op. 1040A10 FUEL TANK - R + R AND DECANT FUEL

- Prepare a suitable container for collecting the engine coolant.

1. Support the weight of the heater unit and loosen the bolts (1a) and the nuts (1b) fixing the independent heater unit mounting bracket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the independent heater coolant inlet and outlet pipes loosening the retaining bands.

Mark one of the two pipes to prevent them being incorrectly refitted.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Remove the additional independent heater water inlet and outlet pipes.

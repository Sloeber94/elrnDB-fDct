Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Remove the the rear wheel.

1. Release the protective boot.

2. Undo the brake caliper fixing bolts.

The bolts fixing the brake caliper are self-locking and should always be replaced each time they are undone or loosened.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Move the caliper (1a) slightly without disconnecting the hydraulic system pipe (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the brake pads.

The pads should be replaced if the thickness of the friction material is less than 1.5 mm.

Check that the same type of brake pads are fitted on each pair of wheels.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the brake pads in position.

- Place the brake caliper in position.

- Tighten the new brake caliper fixing bolts.

- Fit the protective boots.

- Refit the wheel.

- Check that the brakes are working.

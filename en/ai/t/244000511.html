The radio works normally but the radio reception is not as good as previously

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CONNECT OPERATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Use the Connect self-diagnostic function to check for faults in associated systems.

See Test 5580PA CHECK OPERATION OF CONNECT RADIO MODULE

Move on to Step

1. Geographical area found to be incorrect

1. Restore correct geographical area

2. Aerial status found to be in red field.

Move on to Step

3. Communication status found to be in red field (internal error)

3. Replace the Connect unit

2

RECEPTION AREA CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is not present in areas of poor reception. The reception should improved significantly when the vehicle moves away from such areas. If the reception area is very poorly served, attempts to switch between frequencies may be perceived as pauses

Move on to Step

Poor reception

Deactivate the AF function

3

AMPLIFIED AERIAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • aerial coaxial lead continuity
  • power supply to the aerial amplifier and amplifier
  • correct aerial impedence

Move on to Step

1. Aerial lead fault

1. Repair or replace aerial lead

2. Amplifier power supply cable fault

2. Repair or replace supply ead

3. Aerial amplifier fault

3. Replace the aerial amplifier

4. Aerial fault

4. Repair or replace aerial

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

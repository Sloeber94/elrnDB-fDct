Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position.

Disconnect the electrical connection for the injector.

Check the coil resistance is between 4.1 and 5.1 Ohm at 20 °C.

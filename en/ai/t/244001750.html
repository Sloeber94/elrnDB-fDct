Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

Control device H030 allows the selection and movement of the door mirror required, left or right, upper or lower. The device receives an ignition-operated power supply, protected by fuse F27 located in underfacia junction unit B002. Door mirrors P060 and P061 receive controls to move the relevant mirrors:

  • pins 5 and 3: upper mirror movement;
  • pins 2 and 1: lower mirror movement;
  • pin 4 : shared supply supplifed by switch H030.

Control device H030 contains interior lighting that is activated whenever side lights are turned on - pin 5. It is connected to earth C020 via pin 1.

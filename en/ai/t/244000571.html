1060OB LOW FUEL DELIVERY PRESSURE CIRCUIT CHECK

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V, 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Connect the tester pressure transducer between the diesel filter and the pressure pump (connect it to the filter outlet).

Turn the ignition key ON.

Within 30 seconds, check that the pressure on the pressure gauge is between 2 and 3 bars.

The tank pump will go off after 30 seconds.

Make sure that the taps and the adjustment screws at the side of the pressure gauge are open (except the ones on ducts not connected to the system).

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Connect the tester pressure transducer between the diesel filter and the pressure pump (connect it to the filter outlet).

Turn the ignition key ON.

Within 30 seconds, check that the pressure on the pressure gauge is between 0.6 and 0.7 bars.

The tank pump will go off after 30 seconds.

Make sure that the taps and the adjustment screws at the side of the pressure gauge are open (except the ones on ducts not connected to the system).

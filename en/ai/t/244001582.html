Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The earths present on the vehicle, categorised according to their relationship with the various wires, are as follows:

  • C001 Battery earth: wiring 63
  • C002 Battery earth on engine: wiring 40
  • C003 Battery earth on body: wiring 40
  • C010 left front earth; wiring 12
  • C012 ABS front earth: wiring 63
  • C015 Driver's dashboard earth: wiring 20
  • C016 Air conditioning unit earth: wiring 65, 67
  • C020 Passenger side dashboard earth: wiring 19
  • C030 Left rear earth: wiring 23
  • C031 Right rear earth: wiring 24
  • C040 Earth on engine: wiring 50
  • C040B Earth on engine: wiring 50
  • C045 Fan earth: wiring 11, 12
  • C050A Air Bag system earth: wiring 60
  • C050B Air Bag system earth: wiring 61
  • C080 RH rear tail-light earth: wiring 24
  • C081 LH rear tail-light earth: wiring 23
  • C090 Heat flange earth: wiring 50

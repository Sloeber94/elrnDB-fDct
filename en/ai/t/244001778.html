Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The minibar fridge predisposition system operates with the key ON (INT). It is controlled by a dedicated relay located in the optional junction unit under the instrument facia.

The power line from the above relay comes directly from the battery and is protected by a dedicated fuse, also housed in the optional junction unit.

The key-operated line is protected by two dedicated fuses, one housed in the engine bay junction unit and the other in the junction unit under the instrument facia.

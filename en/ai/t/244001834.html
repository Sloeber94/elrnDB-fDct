Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The rev counter is an integral function of the instrument panel. A signal from the engine rpm sensor is sent to the engine control unit, which sends it in turn to the instrument panel. The power supply to the instrument panel is supplied from the control panel. The entire system is protected by dedicated fuses housed in the engine bay and under-facia junction units.

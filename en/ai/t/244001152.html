Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the canister shield and handbrake mounting bracket (1a) and remove the split pin (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

Op. 1076A52 EXHAUST PIPE REAR HEAT SHIELD - R.R.

1. Disconnect the handbrake cable from the rear brakes.

2. Disconnect the handbrake cable from the idler.

3. Using a drill with a suitable helical point, remove the fixing rivets.

4. Remove the linkage rear idler.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the handbrake linkage rear idler and, using a new riveter and new rivets, fasten it to the bodyshell.

- Connect the handbrake cable to the idler.

- Connect the section of the handbrake cable with the rear brakes to the idler.

Op. 1076A52 EXHAUST PIPE REAR HEAT SHIELD - R.R.

- Position the handbrake cable and connect the two sections positioning the split pin.

- Tighten the bolt fixing the canister shield and handbrake mounting bracket.

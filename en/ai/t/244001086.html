Removing

Refitting

- Remove the rear wheel.

1. Release the protective boot.

2. Undo the brake caliper fixing bolts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The bolts fixing the brake caliper are self-locking and should always be replaced each time they are undone or loosened.

1. Remove the brake caliper (1a) from the work area taking care not to damage the oil pip (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. If necessary, remove the brake pads (1), then unto the bolts (1b) and remove the brake back plate (1c).

The bolts fixing the brake back plate are self-locking and should always be replaced each time they are undone or loosened.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the brake disc.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. The minimum permitted thickness for the brake disc after wear is given below; if the figure is below this, the disc should be replaced.

- If there is deterioration or deep grooves, the surfaces of the brake disc can be skimmed using a grinding machine. when the operation is completed the thickness of the brake disc should not be less than the one given below.

- Disc with outer diameter of 280 mm or 300 mm and thickness of 16 mm.

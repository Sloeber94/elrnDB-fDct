1040B42 FILTER/CARBURETTOR LINE - R R

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 1048A10

1. Disconnect the supply pipe from the fuel filter.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Disconnect the supply pipe from the fuel return manifold.

2. Remove the fuel filter supply pipes to the fuel return manifold from the engine compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the fuel filter supply pipes to the fuel return manifold.

- Connect the supply pipe to the fuel filter.

- Connect the supply pipe to the fuel return manifold.

See in Op. 1048A10

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 1048A10

1. Disconnect the rapid connector from the fuel filter.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector from the fuel return manifold.

2. Remove the pipe releasing it from the retaining springs.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the rapid connector to the fuel return manifold.

- Connect the rapid connector to the fuel filter.

- Secure the pipe to the retaining springs.

See in Op. 1048A10

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Removing

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the air supply hose from the heat exchanger to the intake manifold.

- Connect the bands at the two ends of the air pipe from the air/air heat exchanger to the intake manifold.

- Connect the electrical connection for the right front light cluster.

Refitting

1. Open the retaining band (1a) on the rigid air supply pipe and the band (1b) on the heat flange.

2. Remove the hose.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the hose in place.

- Tighten the retaining bands.

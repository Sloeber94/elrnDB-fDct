Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on one of the front brake calipers.

- Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

- Operate the brake pedal until the brake/clutch reservoir is drained.

See in Op. 1048A21

Op. 1048A39 SLEEVE FROM AIR CLEANER TO RIGID PIPE - R R

1. Lift up the spring (1a) and disconnect the pipe (1b) from the clutch release cylinder.

- Release the pipe from the securing bands.

2. Undo the fixing bolts and remove the actuator operating cylinder from the engine compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the clutch release cylinder on the gearbox and tighten the fixing nuts to torque.

- Connect the fluid pipe to the hydraulic clutch release cylinder.

Removing

- Fit the clutch release cylinder on the gearbox and tighten the fixing bolts to torque.

- Connect the fluid pipe to the hydraulic clutch release cylinder.

- Fit the air supply hose to the turbocharger and connect the two ends to the filter casing and to the actual turbocharger.

- Connect the vapour recovery pipe to the air supply hose to the turbocharger.

- Fit the cold intake and intake hose assembly in the engine compartment.

- Connect the intake hose to the air filter casing.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the cold air intake to the upper crossmember.

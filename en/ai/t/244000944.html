Refitting

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the bolt (1a) fixing the ABS mounting bracket (1b) to the crossmember.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts securing the bumper to the crossmember.

2. Undo the nuts (2a) securing the wiring to the crossmember and release it from the fastening springs (2b).

3. Undo the bolts securing the beam to the side struts.

4. Remove the radiator support beam.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the radiator support beam.

- Tighten the bolts securing the beam to the side struts.

Value - daNm

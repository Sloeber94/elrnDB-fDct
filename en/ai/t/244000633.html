Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Disconnect intake hose from the air filter casing and from the cold air intake on the crossmember.

2. Remove the cold intake and intake hose from the engine compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

See in Op. 1048A10

- Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Lower the vehicle and position a hydraulic arm support beneath the gearbox to support the engine.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the gearbox side flexible mounting to the rigid support.

2. Undo the nuts securing the flexible block to the body.

3. Remove the gearbox side power unit flexible mount.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the gearbox side rigid engine support.

- Tighten the nuts fixing the gearbox side flexible mounting to the rigid support.

2. Release and remove the cold air intake hose from the engine compartment.

1. Loosen the bolts (1a), undo the bolts (1b) and remove the air filter mounting bracket (1c).

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Fit the filter mounting bracket and tighten the fixing bolts.

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the bolts securing the cold air intake to the upper beam.

2. Disconnect the intake sleeve from the air cleaner case.

3. Remove the intake sleeve assembly and cold air intake from the engine bay.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts securing the air cleaner case bracket.

2. Undo the bolt securing the air cleaner case to the bracket joining it to the washer fluid reservoir.

3. Disconnect the air delivery sleeve to the turbocharger.

4. Remove the air cleaner case from the engine bay.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

- Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the bolts securing the gearbox side flexible mount to the rigid mount.

2. Undo the nuts securing the flexible block to the body.

3. Remove the gearbox side power unit flexible mount.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

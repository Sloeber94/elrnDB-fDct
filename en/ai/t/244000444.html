1

BATTERY CONNECTIONS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • correct fastening and earth connection of the negative battery terminal;
  • correct fastening of positive battery pole and all leads connected to it;
  • no oxidation at the earth point for the negative battery lead.

See E1010 SUPPLY

Move on to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore the battery connections

See E1010 SUPPLY

Earth lead damaged

Replace the lead

Lead between battery and starter motor damaged

Replace the lead

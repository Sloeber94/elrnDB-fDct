Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: GPL

If this valve is not working properly, the tank could become too full resulting in a dangerous increase in the internal pressure.

If the canister becomes too full when refilling with LPG (which, starting from a gas level of zero, means that the maximum quantity of 45.6 litres of LPG has been exceeded), replace the liquid level restricter safety valve in the canister (80% valve). The nominal capacity of the tank is 57 litres, corresponding to a practical capacity of 45.6 litres.

LPG STORAGE SYSTEM COMPONENTS

Flange

112 mm diameter flange housing the multivalve assembly

LPG supply valve

Operated by a specific solenoid valve, 12V power supply, 1st absorption, closed if there is no electrical supply, sealed, with pressure decrease values that vary between 0.2 and 0.65 bar/h (with a maximum pump capacity of 120 l/h)

LPG return valve

Valve with pressure decrease values that vary between 0.2 and 1.8 bar/h (with a maximum pump capacity of 120 l/h)

Pressurizing valve

Valve with LPG pressurizing prevention when 80% of the maximum tank volume is reached: it takes about 3 minutes to fill a tank with a capacity of around 50 litres

Safety valve

Intervention pressure (tank pressure) less than or equal to 27 bar; allows the controlled escape of LPG

Flow restricter valve

Located inside the supply valve, intervention pressure 0.9 bar (in accordance with regulation E67-01) , it limits the flow rate if the pipes are broken

Electric cable duct

4 pin for pump connection, two for level transducer connection, sealed

LPG pump

Submerged with drip tray and jet pump, complete with filter, 7 - 15V power supply, max absorption 6°, max flow rate 120 l/h, operating temperature range -40°C - 80°C

Level gauge

Reed relay electronic transducer (5 mm reed), 12V power supply, ohmmic resistance with tank empty %gt; 900%Ohgr; ohmmic resistance with tank full %gt; 3000 %Ohgr; temperature operating range -20°C - 80°C, max. operating pressure 70 bar

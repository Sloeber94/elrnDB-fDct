Operational Description

Auxiliary control panel H115 receives an ignition-controlled power supply (INT), from a line protected by fuse F51, located in underfacia junction unit B002.

The auxiliary control panel comes with two specific switches that control activation of courtesy lights G010, G011 and G012. Simultaneous activation of the blue lights (one bulb allocated) of all the courtesy lights is controlled from pin 6 of the panel. Simultaneous activation of the white lights (two bulbs allocated) is controlled from pin 7.

The auxiliar control panel comes with its own internal lighting that turns on when the side lights come on. This control is supplied by control panel H090 - pin 12 connector C -.

The courtesy lights are connected to earth C020 from pin 1 connector D.

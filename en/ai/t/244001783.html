Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicles are fitted with a front power point and also:

  • a rear power point
  • a side power point (for Panorama and Combi versions).

The power point power supply line is protected by a dedicated fuse housed in the optional underfacia junction unit and is controlled by a relay, also housed in the same control unit.

The power points come with internal lighting that is activated when the side lights are turned on.

The power supply directly from the battery is protected by a fuse housed in the underfacia junction unit.

0010T21 COOLANT CHECK DENSITY AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Check that the level of the engine coolant is between the MIN and MAX marks on the coolant reservoir.

This check should be carried out after waiting for a few minutes with the engine switched off and the vehicle on a level surface.

Do not remove the tank cap when the engine is hot as there is a risk of scalding.

- If necessary, top up the level, undoing the cap and introducing engine coolant.

ENGINE COOLING CIRCUIT

Never exceed the MAX level.

- Fit the cap.

- Start up the vehicle and let the engine idle for a few minutes.

- Switch off the engine and wait for a few minutes.

- Check once again that the level of the engine coolant is between the MIN and MAX marks on the coolant reservoir.

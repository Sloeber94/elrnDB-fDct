Removing

- Remove the oil seal from its seat on the intake manifold shaft.

When removing, take care not to scratch the camshaft and its seat.

1. Use the tool to fit a new oil seal to the intake side camshaft. Tighten the tool screw to bring the oil seal into contact with the seat in the head extension.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

When the key is turned to MAR (INT), a power supply reaches relay T29, housed in the optional underfacia junction unit B101, via a line protected by fuse F27, housed in underfacia junction unit B002. When the relay is supplied, it closes the internal contact to supply junction D079 with power directly from the battery via a line protected by fuse F59, housed in optional junction unit B101.

The ignition-operated power supply is protected by fuse F27, housed in underfacia junction unit B002.

Pin 2 of junction D079 is connected to earth C020.

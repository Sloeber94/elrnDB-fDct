Refitting

Drain the supply pipe from the supply solenoid valve to the injectors, following the instructions in the procedure:

Op. 1043A02 LPG SYSTEM LINES PIPE DRAINING OPERATIONS

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical connectors from the LPG injectors.

Description

Connector

1

LPG injector

N073

2. Undo the nuts (2a) and move the petrol injector cable loom duct (2b) aside.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the connector for the rigid return pipe from the injectors, on the injector holder body; keep the seal underneath taking special care not to damage the pipe threaded seat.

2. Undo the connector (2a) joining the rigid pipe with the flexible LPG return pipe (2b) from the injectors; keep the seal underneath taking special care not to damage the pipe threaded seat.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the bands (3a) and remove the rigid return pipe (3b) from the injectors.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Position the LPG rigid return pipe and secure it to the injector holder bracket using the retaining bands.

Screw in and tighten the connector for the rigid return pipe from the injectors to the injector holder body to the recommended torque.

1056B14 THROTTLE CASE - R + R

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 1048A31 RIGID PIPE CONNECTING AIR CLEANER TO INLET MANIFOLD RIGID PIPE R R

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Lower the vehicle.

1. Disconnect the connection for the air temperature sensor.

2. Disconnect the connection for the throttle position sensor.

3. Disconnect the connection for the idle air heater.

4. Disconnect the stepping acutator connection.

5. Acting on the band, release the petrol vapour recovery pipe.

6. Release the engine oil vapour recovery pipe.

7. Disconnect the accelerator bracket.

8. Undo the fixing bolts and remove the throttle casing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the throttle casing in position.

- Tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the accelerator control bracket.

- Connect the engine oil vapour recovery pipe.

- Connect the petrol vapour recovery pipe and renew the band.

- Connect the connection for the idle air heater.

- Connect the connection for the throttle position sensor.

- Connect the connection for the air temperature sensor.

- Connect the stepping acutator connection.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

Op. 1048A31 RIGID PIPE CONNECTING AIR CLEANER TO INLET MANIFOLD RIGID PIPE R R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

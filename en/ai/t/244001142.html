Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

- Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on the rear brake caliper.

- Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the rear brake caliper bleed valve.

- Operate the brake pedal until the brake/clutch reservoir is drained.

1. Undo the connector for the rigid pipe at the rear suspension axle from the support with the rear brake caliper flexible pipe.

2. Release the retaining spring.

3. Undo the connector for the rigid pipe at the rear suspension axle from the connector at the rear suspension axle for the rear brake pipes.

4. Remove the rigid pipe releasing it from the springs.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the rigid pipe at the rear suspension axle is not damaged.

- Fit the rigid pipe on the rear suspension axle and secure it to the springs.

- Tighten the connector for the rigid pipe at the rear suspension axle to the support with the rear brake caliper flexible pipe.

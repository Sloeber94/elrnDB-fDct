Refitting

Undo the repressurizing valve cap and extract it.

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and release the mounting collar (1b)

2. Remove the cap for the repressurizing valve.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Secure the anchorage lead to the cap for the repressurizing valve and secure it using the fixing bolt.

Tighten the cap for the repressurizing valve in its housing.

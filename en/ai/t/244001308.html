5040D21 AIR CONDITIONER CONTROL COVER - R.R.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Supplementary air conditioner

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections.

3. Remove the air conditioning controls trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Supplementary air conditioner

Refitting

Removing

- Move the air conditioning controls trim closer and connect the electrical connections.

- Position the trim and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

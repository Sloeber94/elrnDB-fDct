1044B10 FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY - R + R OR REPLACE

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

1. Disconnect the rapid connectors.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the bracket and remove the fuel filter assembly.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the fuel filter assembly and secure it to the bracket and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Connect the rapid connectors to the filter.

- Lower the vehicle and remove it from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

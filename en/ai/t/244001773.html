Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a front power point and a cigar lighter housed in the middle of the facia next the radio and next to the ashtray respectively. The operation of both is controlled by dedicated relays and protected by fuses located in the underfacia control unit. The direct battery power supply is enabled only with the ignition key ON (INT/A). This strategy has been adopted to provide the engine with maximum power during ignition, due to the high potential power uptake by appliances connected to the point.

The power point and cigar lighter both come with interior lighting that is activated when the side lights are on.

Press the cigar lighter knob to operate the lighter: after a few seconds it will emerge automatically, ready for use.

1040A10 FUEL TANK - R + R AND DECANT FUEL

Path

Applies to

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Draw off the fuel from the tank using a special tool.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the passenger compartment carpet (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts.

2. Remove the protective cover for the submerged pump housing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the submerged pump.

2. Disconnect the fuel supply and return pipes from the rapid connectors.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Open the band and release the tank fuel filler pipe.

2. Undo the lower bolt fixing the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

3. Open the retaining band and disconnect the front anti-evaporation pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

- Position a hydraulic jack with a suitable support under the fuel tank.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the tank to the bodyshell.

2. Lower the hydraulic jack, disconnect the fuel reflux pipe (2a) and remove the fuel tank (2b).

- Disconnect the anti-evaporation pipes acting on the retaining bands.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the fuel tank on the support tool and fit it in the special housing.

- Connect the anti-evaporation pipes and restore the fastenings using the bands.

- Tighten the fuel tank fixing bolts to torque.

Value - daNm

- Remove the hydraulic jack and support from underneath the tank.

- Connect the tank fuel filler pipe.

- Connect the fuel reflux pipe to the tank.

- Connect the electrical connection for the submerged pump.

- Connect the fuel supply and return pipes to the rapid connectors.

- Fit the protective cover for the subemerged pump housing and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the passenger compartment carpet and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

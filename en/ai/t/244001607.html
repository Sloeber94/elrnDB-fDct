Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The main beams are activated by an earth signal from stalk unit H005 - pin 2 connector B - by turning the wheel.

Control panel H090 receies a dipped beam activation signal at pin 16 of connector D, and controls activation of a warning light on control panel E050 - pin 11 connector D -. Control panel H090, via pin 6 of connector A, excites relay T14 located in engine bay junction unit B001, which supplies power to front lights F010 and F011. The line is protected by fuse F30, also located in engine bay junction unit B001.

Junction unit B001 receives a power supply from ignition switch H001 (15/54) and directly from the battery, via protective fuse F70, located in the battery control unit.

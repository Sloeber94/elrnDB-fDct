Removing

Refitting

1. At the bench, disconnect the inlet pipe loosening the fastening bands.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Undo the bolt securing the silencer fastening band to the independent heater unit.

1. Remove the silencer with the drainage pipe for the independent heater unit.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connectors for the wiring from the independent heater.

2. Remove the wiring for the independent heater unit.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the independent heater unit bracket.

2. Separate the mounting bracket from the independent heater unit.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the electrical connections protective cover.

2. Disconnect the electrical connectors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the water pump bracket (1b).

- Remove the band securing the flexible pipe to the pump.

2. Remove the water pump.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the fan assembly.

2. Remove the fan assembly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fasten the fan assembly to the independent heater casing tightening the fixing bolts.

- Place the water pump in the correct fitting position.

- Fit the flexible pipe on the pump and secure it using the band.

- Secure the water pump to the independent heater casing tightening the fixing bolt for the pump retaining band.

- Connect the electrical connectors and fasten the protective cover.

- Secure the independent heater unit mounting bracket to the heater casing tightening the fixing bolts.

- Connect the wiring electrical connectors to the heater unit.

- Fasten the drainage pipe to the heater tightening the securing band using the bolt.

- Fasten the air inlet pipe to the heater using the retaining bands.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Undo the gearbox casing filler plug on the differential.

- Check that the level of the oil just touches the lower edge of the filler opening.

- If necessary, top up the oil in the gearbox casing via the filler opening.

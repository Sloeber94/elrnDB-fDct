Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two extra-bright rear fog lamps housed in the right and left tail-lights that can be used when visibility is poor.

The rear fog lamps are activated by means of a button on the control panel.

Enablement for rear fog lamp activation is dependent on the use of the dipped beams or fog lamps.

Reversing light activation is indicated by a warning light on the control switch.

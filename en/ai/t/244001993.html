Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The basic air conditioner is complemented by a supplementary air conditioner to offer all passengers comfortable environmental conditions by taking in cool air even when temperature and relative humidity are high.

The supplementary air condition is activated with the basic air conditioner by means of a specific button on the air conditioning system control panel. Special adjustable, positionable direct the air flow toward passengers in the central and rear seating rows.

The supplementary air conditioner is activated automatically when the basic air conditioner is turned on.

The power supplies are protected by special fuses in the engine bay junction unit, underfacia control unit and optional underfacia control unit.

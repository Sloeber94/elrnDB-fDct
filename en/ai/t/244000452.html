PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • condition of the air intake circuit;
  • that there are no faults in the air filter;
  • the seal of the diesel pipes;
  • no obstructions in the catalytic converter;
  • no water, petrol, paraffin or dirt in the fuel tank and filter;
  • battery earth terminal fastening.

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • the leads are properly fastened to the battery;
  • wiring on engine correctly fastened to earth;
  • correct installation of LPG injector delivery and return pipes;
  • LPG system seal using an electronic leak detector to identify gas leaks;
  • Intake side air leaks downstream of throttle body;
  • leaks from exhaust manifold
  • valve seal/valve clearance by carrying out cylinder compression test;
  • camshaft timing;
  • LPG filters

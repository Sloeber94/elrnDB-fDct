1. Undo the bolt (1a) and move the brake pipe support (1b).

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and move the ABS sensor (2b).

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the support (3b).

4. Undo the stub axle fixing bolts.

5. Remove the stub axle.

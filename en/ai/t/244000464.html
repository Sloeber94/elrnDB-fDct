Applies to: all engines; all production periods

One dipped headlamp does not work when the stalk switch wheel is operated with the key ON.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

INDIVIDUAL LIGHT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the bulbs and associated bulb holder;

Move on to Step

1. Bulbs damaged

1. Change the bulbs

Op. 5540B25 DIPPED HEADLAMP BEAM (1), LH OR RH - R R

2. Front light cluster fault

2. Repair or replace the front light cluster

Op. 5540B10 EXTERIOR LIGHT DIMMER RESISTOR - R+R

2

DIPPED HEADLAMP ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Dipped headlamp fuses
  • Bulb earth

Move on to Step

1. Component defective

1. Replace defective component (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

2. Connections interrupted

2. Repair the connections

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

3

ENGINE BAY CONTROL UNIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Correct power supply to engine bay control unit;
  • Check that battery output voltages are generated to control the bulbs when an earth signal is activated from the switch control unit to the engine bay control unit

Move on to Step

1. Connections interrupted

1. Repair the connections

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

2. Engine bay control unit fault

2. Change engine bay control

Op. 5505A13 SUPPLEMENTARY JUNCTION CONTROL UNIT IN ENGINE BAY - R R

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the passenger compartment carpet (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts.

2. Remove the submerged pump housing protective cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Disconnect the fuel supply pipes from the rapid connector.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

- Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Release the supply pipe from the fastenings springs and release the pipe from the tank.

- Lower the lift.

See in Op. 1048A10

1. Disconnect the supply pipe from the fuel filter.

- Release it from the securing bands on the bulkhead.

2. Remove the single fuel supply pipes from the upper part of the engine compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the single fuel supply pipes in the upper part of the engine compartment.

- Connect the supply pipe to the fuel filter.

- Fasten it to the securing bands on the bulkhead.

See in Op. 1048A10

- Raise the lift.

- Position the supply pipe in the housing on the tank passing it between the bodyshell and the tank.

- Fasten the pipe to the springs on the underbody.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Lower the vehicle.

- Working from inside the passenger compartment, connect the supply pipe to the rapid connector on the pump.

- Fit the submerged pump housing protective cover.

- Fit the passenger compartment carpet and fasten it to the bodyshell using the fixing bolts.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

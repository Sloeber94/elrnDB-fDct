1. Remove the junction unit mounting bracket.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the fuel filler cap.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing nut located under the brake servo.

- Release the filler pipe from the bands securing the electrical wiring.

- Raise the lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

2. Undo the lower bolts fixing the filler pipe to the bodyshell.

3. Disconnect the flexible section of the fuel filler pipe (3a) and the reflux pipe (3b) from the tank.

- Lower the lift.

4. Separate the two sections of the pipe working on the retaining band.

5. Remove the pipe in two sections.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. With the filler pipe at the bench, remove the filler internal plastic protection.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the plate with the threaded connector for the fuel cap from the filler pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the plate with the threaded connector for the fuel cap on the filler pipe.

- Fit the filler internal plastic protection.

- Fit the tank section of the fuel filler pipe and then connect the next section securing them with the retaining band.

- Raise the lift.

- Connect the flexible section of the fuel filler pipe (3a) and the reflux pipe (3b) to the tank.

- Tighten the lower bolts fixing the filler pipe to the bodyshell.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Lower the lift.

- Secure the electrical wiring to the filler pipe.

- Tighten the fixing nut located under the brake servo.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

- Secure the wiring to the filler pipe.

- Fit the junction unit mounting bracket.

- Fit the engine compartment junction unit mounting and tighten the fixing nuts.

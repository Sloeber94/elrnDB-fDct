1. Remove the bands (1a) and (1b) retaining the heater radiator supply and return pipes.

2. Undo the retaining bolts.

- Open the tap control cable retaining spring.

3. Disconnect the tap control cable and remove it.

4. Disconnect the heater radiator supply and return pipes (4a) and (4b).

5. Remove the tap.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the tap control cable and secure it to the fastening spring.

- Position the tap and connect the heater radiator supply and return pipes.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the tap.

- Secure the heater radiator supply and return pipes using suitable retaining bands.

Check the condition of the seal and replace it if necessary.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7005D22 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS GUIDE (ONE) - R R WITH DOOR PANEL (AND SUPPORT FRAME) REMOVED

1. Remove the spring securing the lock barrel.

2. Remove the lock barrel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the lock barrel in the special housing in the door and in the lock.

- Fasten the lock barrel to the door, securing it with the retaining spring.

Op. 7005D22 FRONT SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS GUIDE (ONE) - R R WITH DOOR PANEL (AND SUPPORT FRAME) REMOVED

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

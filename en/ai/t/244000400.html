Fuel supply manifold

Petrol injectors

Fuel pressure regulator

Fuel pressure is assumed as a fixed parameter (not controllable by the control unit) that is essential for calculating fuel quantity: therefore the regulator should never be tampered with or else the reliability of the engine and the catalytic converter will be adversely affected.

Engine speed sensor

Operating principle

The sensor gap angular position cannot be adjusted.

Throttle position sensor

The sensor angular position cannot be adjusted.

Absolute pressure sensor

If replaced, the connection pipe between vacuum intake and sensor must be the same length and diameter as the original to ensure correct operation during transitories.

Engine timing sensor

The sensor angular position cannot be adjusted.

Throttle body

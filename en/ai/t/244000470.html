Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The reversing lights do not come on when reverse is engaged with the key ON.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

INDIVIDUAL LIGHT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check condition of the individual bulbs and associated bulb holder unit

Move on to Step

1. Bulbs damaged

1. Change the bulbs

Op. 5540A74 BULB OF A SIDELIGHT OR FOG LAMP OR REVERSING LIGHT IN A TAIL-LIGHT, LH OR RH R R

2. Bulb holder unit fault

2. Repair or replace the tail-light

Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

REVERSING LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Reversing light fuse;
  • onnections between battery, ignition switch, underfacia junction unit and reversing light switch;
  • connections betwee reversing light switch and bulbs;
  • bulb earth

Move on to Step

1. Fuse blown

1. Change the fuse (find out what caused it to blow)

2. Connections interrupted

2. Repair the connections

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

3. Bulb earth defective

3. Repair the earth contact

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

3

REVERSING SWITCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the reversing light switch is properly fastened and operational

Move on to Step

Reversing light switch operation

Repair or replace the reversing light switch

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

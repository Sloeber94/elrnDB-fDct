Removing

Refitting

1. Unscrew the bolts securing the top part of the manifold pipe.

2. Raise the top part of manifold pipe (2a) and remove injector (2b).

- Remove the other three injectors.

During removal, mark the position of the injector (relevant cylinder number) in relation to the numbering on the single manifold pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Check the condition of the injector seals.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

- Fit the injectors to the manifold and position in relation to the numbering on the manifold.

- Position the top part of the manifold pipe and tighten the retaining bolts.

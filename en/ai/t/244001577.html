Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

All electrical systems and circuits are supplied by battery A001.

The power supply to the various devices are managed by engine bay control unit B001, underfacia control unit B002 and, where fitted, by optional underfacia control unit (B101), which contain fuses and relays that allow correct operation of the electrical system.

Other circuits are supplied by turning the ignition key H001 to various positions:

The entire system is protected by fuses housed in battery control unit B099.

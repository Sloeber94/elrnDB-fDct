Diagram for checking underbody

This chapter is designed to highlight the typical vehicle measurements in order to work correctly when carrying out repairs and produce the best possible results. The comparative measurements, expressed in millimetres, may be subject to differences (of around 2mm) which the experienced mechanic will be able to interpret and establish whether they are due to any impact or to manufacturing tolerances.

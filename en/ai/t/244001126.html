Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

1. Working underneath the vehicle, undo the connectors for the rigid brake pipe and let the hydraulic fluid drain into a suitable container.

2. Undo the connector for the rigid pipe.

3. Remove the rigid pipe (3a) releasing it from the springs (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the rigid pipe is not damaged.

- Position the rigid pipe and fit it to the springs.

- Tighten the rigid pipe connectors.

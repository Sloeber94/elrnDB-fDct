1076B20 REAR EXHAUST SILENCER - R + R

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the exhaust silencers to the front section.

2. Release the flexible mounting (2a) from the support bearing (2b).

3. Undo the bolt fixing the intermediate flexible mounting to the bodyshell.

4. Undo the fixing bolts (4a) and remove the rear exhaust silencer (4b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the exhaust silencers.

Tighten the bolts fixing the rear support to the bodyshell.

Secure the front flexible mounting to the pin on the bodyshell.

Tighten the bolts fixing the exhaust silencers to the front section to torque.

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on the lift.

Secure the exhaust catalyzer to the bodyshell before separating the rear exhaust pipe.

Place a hydraulic support under the rear exhaust silencer.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the brackets with the flexible buffer (1b) supporting the rear exhaust silencer.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) fixing the catalyzer to the rear section of the exhaust silencer and keep the collar (2b).

3. Lower the hydraulic support and remove the rear exhaust silencer.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: Methane and GPL

Removing

Refitting

Op. 2410A18 TRANSMISSION WITH JOINTS INCLUDED - R.R.

- Unscrew the grease fitting for the universal joint securing the front propeller shaft to the viscous coupling flange.

1. Unscrew the bots securing the propeller shaft universal joint to the viscous coupling flange.

2. Undo the bolts securing the rear transmission shaft sleeve to the viscous coupling assembly.

3. Remove the viscous coupling assembly

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V, 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: 4X4

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the sleeve with the propeller shaft in place and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the propeller shaft universal joint in its seat on the viscous coupling and tighten the bolts.

- Tighten the universal joint grease fitting.

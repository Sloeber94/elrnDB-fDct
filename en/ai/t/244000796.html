Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the hose from the rigid section of the air supply pipe to the heat exchanger and from the rigid section of the air pipe from the turbocharger, then remove the air supply hose between the turbocharger and the heat exchanger.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the hose connecting it to the rigid supply sections from the turbocharger and the air supply to the heat exchanger.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the retaining band (1b) on the heat exchanger and the band (1b) on the turbocharger rigid pipe.

2. Remove the hose.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the hose in position.

- Tighten the retaining bands.

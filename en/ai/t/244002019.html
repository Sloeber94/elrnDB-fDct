Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The radio system is fully managed by the Connect system and consists of four speakers:

  • two on the front doors;
  • two on the facia;

The Connect system receives power directly from the battery and via the ignition. It is protected by dedicated fuses housed in the engine bay and underfacia junction units. The system is activated automatically when the ignition key is turned to MAR. When the system is turned on, it assumes the status that was active when it was last turned off.

The following operations may be carried out by means of steering wheel controls:

  • volume control;
  • MUTE function;
  • voice recognition;
  • frequency range selection;
  • frequency or CD track selection;
  • phone call management.

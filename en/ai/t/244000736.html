1040A32 LABEL NOT AVAILABLE

Path

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the lower bolt fixing the filler pipe to the bodyshell.

2. Act on the retaining bands (2a) and release and remove the fuel filler pipe hose (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the hose joining the filler pipe to the tank to the tank and to the filler pipe.

- Restore the fastenings removed previously.

- Tighten the lower bolts fixing the filler pipe to the bodyshell.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Raise the lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the lower bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

2. Disconnect the hose joining the filler pipe to the tank from the tank and from the filler pipe, then remove it .

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the hose joining the filler pipe to the tank to the tank and to the filler pipe, then remove it .

- Tighten the lower bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe to the bodyshell.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Lower the lift.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

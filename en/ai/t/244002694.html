1057B20 LPG/G SWITCH

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Release the left side vent.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the LPG/petrol selector switch to the trim.

3. Disconnect the electrical connection.

4. Remove the LPG/petrol selector switch from the housing in the trim.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the LPG/petrol selector switch and tighten the fixing bolts.

Connect the electrical connection for the switch.

Fit the left side vent and check that it is secured.

Connect the negative battery terminal.

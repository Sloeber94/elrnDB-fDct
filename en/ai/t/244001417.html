Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the water shield protection.

The operation of removing the water shield is destructive. To prevent damaging penetration, a new shield must be used when refitting.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Clean any residues of adhesive from the door frame.

- Check that the new protection is intact and not torn.

- Position the wiring in the special housings in the water shield.

- Position the new water shield protection, taking care to ensure that the adhesive sticks to the door frame.

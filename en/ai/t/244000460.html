I02 PARKING LIGHTS NOT WORKING

Path

Kind

Interactive page

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The parking lights do not work under any circumstances.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The driver turns on the parking lights by turning the key anticlockwise and pressing the special release button.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

INDIVIDUAL LIGHT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the bulbs and associated bulb holder;

Move on to Step

1. Front bulbs damaged

1. Change the bulbs

Op. 5540B28 BULB (1) FOR FRONT TURN SIGNAL IN A HEAD LAMP (1), LH OR RH - R R

2. Rear bulbs damaged

2. Change the bulbs

Op. 5540A74 BULB OF A SIDELIGHT OR FOG LAMP OR REVERSING LIGHT IN A TAIL-LIGHT, LH OR RH R R

3. Tail-light fault

3. Repair or replace the tail-light

Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

4. Front light cluster fault

4. Repair or change the front light cluster

Op. 5540B10 EXTERIOR LIGHT DIMMER RESISTOR - R+R

5. Number plate light fault

5. Repair or change the number plate lights

Op. 5540A94 L/H OR R/H NUMBER PLATE LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

SIDE LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • side lights fuses;
  • connections between battery, engine bay control unit, ignition switch and underfacia junction unit;
  • connections between underfacia junction unit and bulbs;
  • bulb earth

Move on to Step

1. Component defective

1. Replace defective component (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

2. Connections interrupted

2. Repair the connections

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

3

IGNITION SWITCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for a battery voltage, particularly toward the underfacia junction unit to supply the side/number plate lights, when the key is turned anticlockwise and the release button is pressed

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Ignition switch fault

Repair or change the ignition switch

Op. 5520A12 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR BAG

4

UNDERFACIA JUNCTION UNIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for a battery voltage to power the bulbs at the underfacia junction unit output when the key is turned anticlockwise and the release button is pressed

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Underfacia junction unit fault

Repair or change the underfacia junction unit

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

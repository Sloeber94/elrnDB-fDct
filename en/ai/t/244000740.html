1040A14 FUEL TANK - R + R FOR REPLACEMENT

Removing

Refitting

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

See in Op. 1040A10

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the ring nut (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Raise the submerged pump, release the perimeter seal (1a) from its housing, then remove the fuel pump (1b) complete with level gauge.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

1. Open the band (1a) and remove the hose joining the filler pipe and the tank (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the hose joining the filler pipe and the tank.

- Position the fuel pump, complete with new gasket, in its housing in the tank.

- Tighten the nuts fixing the fuel pump ring nut to the tank.

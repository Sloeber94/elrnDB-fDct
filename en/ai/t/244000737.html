1040A40 PLUG FOR FUEL FILLER PIPE - R + R

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Open the fuel flap.

- Open the filler plug using a suitable spanner.

1. Undo the fuel filler plug, release the retaining pin (1a) from its housing and remove the actual plug (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the filler plug in its housing and tighten it.

- Secure the retaining pin in its housing on the flap.

- Close the filler plug using a suitable spanner.

- Close the fuel flap.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

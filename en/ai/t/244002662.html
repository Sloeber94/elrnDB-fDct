Removing

Refitting

Drain the supply pipe from the supply solenoid valve to the injectors, following the instructions in the procedure:

Op. 1043A02 LPG SYSTEM LINES PIPE DRAINING OPERATIONS

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the pressure sensor electrical connector.

Description

Connector

1

LPG pressure sensor

K053

2. Undo the connector for the flexible supply pipe to the injectors on the intermediate block.

Loosen the connectors for the rigid supply pipe on the solenoid valve and on the intermediate block.

3. Undo the nuts fixing the intermediate connector to the mounting bracket.

4. Undo the connector (4a) for the rigid supply pipe for the solenoid valve; then remove the intermediate block (4b) complete with LPG pressure sensor.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1084B30 ENGINE OIL FILTER R R

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

1. Undo the fixing bolts.

2. Remove the inspection cover located on the guard under the engine.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

- Position a suitable container under the oil filter to collect the engine oil.

1. Remove the engine oil cartridge filter.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Tighten the oil filter by hand and lubricate the filter seal with engine oil.

- Check the quantity of oil in the engine.

- Fit the inspection cover and tighten the fixing bolts.

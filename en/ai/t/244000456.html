The engine cuts out unexpectedly whilst driving. It works later on after starting normally.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • presence of fuel in the tank;
  • wiring on engine correctly fastened to earth;
  • battery correctly fastened to earth;
  • positive battery pole and all leads connected to it correctly fastened;
  • no water in the fuel filter.

Introduce fuel

0520G65 ACCELERATOR CONTROL PEDAL - ADJUST END TRAVEL WITH FDC

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Connect the tester station (EXAMINER or SDC) to the socket in the engine compartment.

- Move the accelerator pedal to the end of its travel (fully depressed).

- Record the accelerator pedal potentiometer output voltage.

Subject

Value

.

Accelerator pedal

Volt

-

Potentiometer output voltage

3.5 ÷ 4.4

- If the value recorded is not within the recommended limits, replace the accelerator pedal complete with potentiometer.

- Disconnect the diagnostic equipment socket from the connector in the engine compartment.

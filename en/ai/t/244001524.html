Removing

Refitting

- Remove the protective cap for the lower seat belt fixing bolt.

1. Undo the lower seat belt fixing bolt.

2. Undo the upper bolts fixing the trim.

- Release the seat belt from the trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the lower centre pillar trim element.

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the seat belt in the trim element.

- Place the trim element in the special housing.

- Fasten the trim element tighten the lower seat belt fixing bolt to the recommended torque.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two specific dipped beam headlamps.

The dipped beams are activated by turning a wheel on the stalk switch to the next position after the side light setting (second position).

The dipped beams stay on even with the main beams on and are turned of by turning on the fog lamps.

The circuit is protected by two special fuses: one for the right headlamp and one for the left.

The dipped beam circuit also enables the operation of certain other functions, e.g. rear fog lamp activation and headlamp alignment control.

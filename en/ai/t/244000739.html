1040A74 LABEL NOT AVAILABLE

Path
Procedures > 10 ENGINE UNIT > 1040 FUEL TANK AND PIPES > 1040A FUEL TANK AND COMPONENTS > 1040A74 LABEL NOT AVAILABLE
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fDct/en/#/244000739

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection (1a) and undo the fixing nuts (1b), then remove the inertia switch assembly and bracket (1c).

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and separate the inertia switch (2b) from the bracket.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the inertia switch to the bracket.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the inertia switch to the bracket.

- Fit the inertia switch in place complete with bracket.

- Tighten the nuts fixing the inertia switch mounting bracket.

- Connect the electrical connection for the inertia switch.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V, 2.3 JTD 16V, 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

The spark plug warning light does not come with the ignition on;

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V, 2.3 JTD 16V, 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

The warning lights take the form of leds built into the electronic circuit and are NOT therefore replaceable

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V, 2.3 JTD 16V, 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

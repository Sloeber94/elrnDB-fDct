Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The rear window and door mirror demister coils are activated by a specific button on the control panel.

The demister circuit is protected by specific fuses and controlled by a relay in the optional junction unit under the instrument facia.

The control is then deactivated in two ways:

  • MANUALLY: pressing the same button again;
  • AUTOMATICALLY: after 20 minutes effective operation, i.e. with the control activated.

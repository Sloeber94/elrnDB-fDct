Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the driveshaft in a vice.

1. Remove the circlip (1a) and release the three-lobe joint (1b) from the driveshaft.

2. Remove the retaining band (2a) and move the protective boot (2b) back.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the driveshaft is not distorted or offset in the area where the joint is fitted and that the area in contact with the seal is not worn.

- Fit the three-lobe joint on the driveshaft in the special housing securing it using the special flexible clip.

- Fill the protective boot and the bearing with suitable grease.

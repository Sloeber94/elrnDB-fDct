Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The power supply directly from the battery reaches optional junction unit B101 - pin A - from underfacia junction unit B002, via a line protected by fuse F31. Relay T13, also housed in the same control unit, is excited with the ignition key ON (INT/A). Power reaches power points P052 and P158 (the latter only present on panorama and combi versions) - pin 2 - via fuse F64.

The power points come with internal lighting that is activated when the side lights are turned on.

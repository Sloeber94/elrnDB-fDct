Operational Description

Parking sensor M130 is supplied with key-on - at pins 6 and 11 - by a line protected by fuses F27 and F35, located in under-facia junction unit B002.

The remaining control unit pins are connected as follows:

  • pin 1, 2: power supply and earth of parking TV camera
  • pin 3 : composite video signal input from parking TV camera K150;
  • pin 4 : composite video signal earth reference from parking TV camera K150;
  • pin 5 : earth connection C020;
  • pin 7 : led/acoustic signal unit power supply E080;
  • pin 8 : led control of led/acoustic signal unit E080;
  • pin 9 : buzzer control of led/acoustic signal unit E080;

Control unit M130 receives a vehicle speed signal supplied by instrument panel E050 - pin 13 connector E - at pin 10.

Control unit M130 receives a left turn signal activated signal from control panel H090 - pin 1 connector C - at pin 12.

Control panel H090 receives a left turn signal activation earth signal directly from stalk unit H005 - pin 6 connector C -.

Refitting

Drain the supply pipe from the supply solenoid valve to the injectors, following the instructions in the procedure:

Op. 1043A02 LPG SYSTEM LINES PIPE DRAINING OPERATIONS

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the connector (1a) for the supply pipe to the intermediate block (1b) for the supply line; keep the seal underneath taking special care not to damage the pipe threaded seat.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Undo the connector (1a) on the LPG injector body (1b); keep the seal underneath taking special care not to damage the pipe threaded seat.

2. Remove the flexible supply pipe from the connector to the injectors.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Removing

Loosen/tighten brake pipe connectors

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the connector for the rigid pipe from the connector with the rear section of the pipe.

- Release the rigid pipe from the springs securing it to the bodyshell.

2. Remove the rigid pipe from the master cylinder to the rear brake pipe connector via the lower part of the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the rigid pipe from the master cylinder to the rear brake pipe connector via the lower part of the engine compartment.

- Tighten the connector joining the front and rear sections of the rigid pipe.

- Connect the pipe to the springs near the fuel tank.

- Lower the vehicle.

- Connect the connector for the rear brake pipe to the master cylinder.

D25 ENGINE DOES NOT SWITCH TO GAS

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

The engine starts normally in petrol mode but does not then switch to methane mode.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: BIPOWER

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check, first, for the presence of METHANE in the tank by checking methane pressure at the reduction unit intake

Move on to Step 1

Pressure lower than 15 bar and methane level therefore too low

Restore correct methane level

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the following at the petrol and METHANE engine management unit, in the errors ambient:

  • no errors in the petrol and METHANE management control unit

Move on to Step 2

Error detected in the petrol engine management unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Error detected in the METHANE engine management unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

No communication between the petrol engine management unit and METHANE engine management unit

Restore connection between petrol and METHANE control unit

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

METHANE control unit not connected to earth

Restore earth connection

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

2

CHECK METHANE INJECTORS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check methane injectors are operating correctly

See Test 1058A GAS CIRCUIT MULTI POINT INJECTION

Move on to Step 3

Methane injectors defective

Replace the injectors

Op. 1058A68 SET OF CNG INJECTION SYSTEM INJECTORS - R R WITH CNG MANIFOLD PIPE REMOVED INCLUDES GASKET REPLACEMENT

Injector control relay defective

Replace the relay

3

CHECK METHANE/PETROL SELECTION BUTTON

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic connector check the following at the engine control unit, in the parameters ambient :

  • correct operation of the METHANE/Petrol selector

Check operation of the METHANE/Petrol selector

See Test 1058O CNG SUPPLY TESTS

Move on to Step 4

METHANE/PETROL selector defective

Replace the switch

Op. 1058A20 CNG/METHANE SWITCH - R.R.

Wiring broken

Restore wiring

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Selector power supply interrupted

Restore wiring continuity

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Methane control unit defective

Replace METHANE control unit

Op. 1058A80 CNG INJECTION UNIT CONTROL UNIT - R R

4

CHECK METHANEN PRESSURE SIGNAL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • correct power supply to methane pressrue sensor;
  • the correct voltage signal for the methane level sensor to the methane control unit, by carrying out Test 1058AA

See Test 1058A GAS CIRCUIT MULTI POINT INJECTION

Move on to Step 5

Methane pressure sensor power supply incorrect

Restore correct power supply

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Pressure sensor defective

Replace pressure sensor

Op. 1058A44 CNG/FUEL SWITCH - R R

5

CHECK CAN LINE CONNECTING ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check continuity/insulation of CAN line connecting petrol engine control unit and methane engine management unit

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

End of diagnosis

CAN line interrupted

Restore continuity

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

CAN line short-circuited

Restore insulation

See E5032 METHANE ENGINE ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Methane control unit defective

Replace control unit

Op. 1058A80 CNG INJECTION UNIT CONTROL UNIT - R R

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: GPL

The engine starts normally in petrol mode but does not then switch to GAS mode.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: GPL

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check, first of all, that there is LPG in the tank

Move on to Step 1

LPG level low

Restore correct LPG level

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the following at the petrol and LPG engine management unit:

  • in the errors ambient no errors in the petrol and LPG management control units;
  • in the parameters ambient, the plausibility of all the parameters

Move on to Step 2

Error detected in the petrol engine management unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Error detected in the LPG engine management unit

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

No communication between the petrol engine management unit and LPG engine management unit

Restore connection between petrol and LPG control unit

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG control unit not connected to earth

Restore earth connection

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG pressure more than or equal to 20 bar

Move on to Step 6

2

GAS/PETROL SELECTOR CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic connector check the following at the engine control unit, in the parameters ambient :

  • correct operation of the GAS/Petrol selector.

Check the operation of the GAS/Petrol selector by carrying out Test 1057OA

See Test 1057O LGP SUPPLY TESTS

Move on to Step 3

GAS/PETROL selector defective

Replace the selector

Wiring broken

Restore wiring

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Selector power supply interrupted

Restore wiring continuity

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG control unit defective

Replace LPG control unit

3

CHECK CAN LINE CONNECTING ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check continuity/insulation of CAN line connecting petrol engine control unit and LPG engine management unit

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Move on to Step 4

CAN line interrupted

Restore continuity

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

CAN line short-circuited

Restore insulation

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

4

LPG LEVEL SENSOR CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • correct power supply to LPG level sensor;
  • the correct operation of the LPG level sensor, by carrying out Test 1043AE

See Test 1043A GPL TANK

Move on to Step 5

LPG level sensor power supply not correct

Restore correct power supply

See E5031 LPG FUEL SYSTEM ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

LPG level sensor defective

Replace the LPG level sensor

5

CHECK LPG PRESSURE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • the correct operation of the LPG pressure sensor, by carrying out Test 1043BB;
  • the correct operation of the LPG return solenoid valve, by carrying out Test 1043BC;
  • the correct operation of the LPG pump, by carrying out Test 1043AA

See Test 1043B GPL FUEL CIRCUIT PIPES

See Test 1043A GPL TANK

Move on to Step 6

Pressure sensor defective

Replace the sensor

Return solenoid defective

Replace the solenoid valve

LPG pump defective

Replace the LPG pump

6

CHECK ON TANK FILLER RESTRICTER VALVE AND LPG LEVEL SENSOR FLOAT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Disconnect the LPG tank, drain it, send it to the authorized service centre and check:

  • the correct operation of the tank filling restricter valve, by carrying out Test 1043AF;
  • the correct operation of the LPG level sensor, by carrying out Test 1043AE

See Test 1043A GPL TANK

End of diagnosis

Filler restricter valve faulty

Replace the tank filler restricter valve

Level sensor float defective

Replace the sensor

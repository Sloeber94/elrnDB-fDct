Refitting

Removing

- Fit the relay cover.

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the retaining nuts.

2. Remove the cover from the housing on the mounting bracket.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the cover for the EGR valve modulator and the pre-heating control unit.

Tighten the retaining nuts.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the retaining springs (1a) and release the single-acting valve pipe (1b) on the brake servo, undo the fixing nuts (1c).

2. Remove the protective cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Place the protective cover in position.

Tighten the retaining nuts.

Fit the single-acting valve pipe on the retaining springs.

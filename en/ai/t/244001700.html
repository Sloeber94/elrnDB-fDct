Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with one front courtesy light and two rear courtesy lights, one for each row of passengers. The courtesy lights are activated via an auxiliary control panel located to the left of the instrument facia.

The auxiliary control panel comes with two special switches that turn on the courtesy light bulbs separately.

The system is protected by specific fuses housed in the junction unit under the instrument facia.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

Both door mirrors are adjusted using a joystick-typed control located near the mirror. The upper mirror section and/or lower mirror section may be adjusted within each door mirror. This is made possible by a switch that selects the door mirror (right or left) and the mirror section (upper or lower).

The button controls two electrical motors located in each of the two mirrors: a motor controls movements of the upper mirror (horizontal and vertical). The other controls movements of the lower mirror section (horizontal and vertical).

The door mirror adjustment circuit is protected by a dedicated fuse located in the junction unit under the instrument panel.

Adjustment is possible only with the key ON (INT).

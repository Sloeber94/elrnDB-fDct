1008C32 GEARBOX LOWER REACTION ROD ' -R.R.

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Undo the bolt fixing the lower reaction rod to the suspension crossmember.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the lower reaction rod to the mounting bracket on the gearbox.

- Move the power unit slightly forward to make enough room to get the reaction link out.

3. Remove the lower reaction link.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the lower reaction rod in the housing and tighten the bolt fixing the suspension crossmember to torque.

- Tighten the nut fixing the reaction rod to the gearbox to torque.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

1. Unscrew the bolt securing the lower reaction link to the suspension beam.

2. Unscrew the bolt securing the lower reaction link to the anchorage bracket on the gearbox.

- Move the power unit slightly forward to make enough room to get the reaction link out.

3. Remove the lower reaction link.

Applies to: engines: 2.3 JTD 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the lower reaction link in its seat and tighten the bolt securing it to the suspension beam to torque.

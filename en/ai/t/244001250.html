Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Raise the vehicle on a lift.

- Position a suitable container for collecting the fuel.

- Position a suitable container for collecting the coolant.

1. Support the weight of the heater unit and undo the bolts (1a) and the nuts (1b) fixing the independent heater unit mounting bracket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the fuel inlet pipe loosening the fastening band.

2. Disconnect the coolant inlet and outlet pipes loosening the fastening bands.

Mark one of the two pipes to prevent them being incorrectly connected when refitting.

3. Disconnect the electrical connector for the independent heater unit and release it from the mounting bracket.

Removing

Refitting

1. Open the retaining band (1a) on the heat exchanger and the band (1b) on the inlet hose.

2. Undo the fixing bolt.

3. Remove the rigid pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the hose in place.

- Tighten the fixing bolt.

- Fit the hose at both ends.

- Position the retaining bands and tighten them.

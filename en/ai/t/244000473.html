One rear foglamp does not work when the stalk switch wheel is turned with the key ON.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods; only with equipment: Minibus and Van and Panorama and Combi

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

INDIVIDUAL LIGHT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the bulb and associated bulb holder;

Move on to Step

1. Bulb damaged

1. Change the bulbs

Op. 5540A74 BULB OF A SIDELIGHT OR FOG LAMP OR REVERSING LIGHT IN A TAIL-LIGHT, LH OR RH R R

2. Light cluster fault

2. Repair or replace front light cluster

Op. 5540A44 L/H OR R/H TAIL LIGHT (ONE) - R+R

2

REAR FOGLAMP ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Connections between switch control unit and bulbs;
  • Bulb earth

Move on to Step

Connections interrupted

Repair the connections

See E2031 REAR FOG LAMPS

3

SWITCH CONTROL UNIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Correct power supply to switch control unit
  • Check that a battery voltage output is generated to control the rear fog lamp bulbs when the rear fog lamp button is activated

Move on to Step

1. Power supply incorrect

1. Restore correct power supply

See E2031 REAR FOG LAMPS

2. Switch control unit fault

2. Replace switch control unit

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

TRANSMISSION UNIT

4 wheel drive

Four wheel drive that can be engaged manually.

Rear wheel propeller shaft in two sections.

Crown wheel and differential unit built into the gearbox casing.

Rear differential that can be locked through a button in the cab.

MAINTENANCE

Idler - Rear differential

1st oil change within 1,500 - 2,00 km;

check oil every 7,500 km;

change oil every 30,000 km.

CAPACITIES

Idler: 0.5 litres

Rear differential: 2.3 litres

Fuel tank capacity: 70 litres

Including a reserve of around: 3.5 - 5.5 litres

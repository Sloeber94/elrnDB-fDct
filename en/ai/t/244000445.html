When the key is turned to the ON position, the instruments light up but the starter motor does not turn.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Turn the key ON and check that the generator comes on (it should then go off when the engine is running)

Move on to Step 1

The generator does not come on

See table K40 BATTERY CHARGING WARNING LIGHT NOT WORKING

1

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connections between battery, ignition switch and starter motor.

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Move on to Step 2

Connections not correct

Restore battery connections

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Earth lead damaged

Replace the lead

Lead between battery and starter motor damaged

Replace the lead

Op. 5530B26 LEAD BETWEEN BATTERY AND STARTER MOTOR - R+R

2

CHECK STARTER MOTOR CONNECTIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check connections between:

  • battery, ignition switch and motor control relay;
  • battery and motor control relay;
  • relay, switch for remote control switch and starter motor.

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Move on to Step 3

Connections not correct

Repair the connections

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Relay faulty

Replace the relay

Switch faulty

Replace the switch

3

IGNITION SWITCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the ignition switch and that it is working properly

Move on to Step 4

Ignition switch fault

Repair or replace the ignition switch

Op. 5520A12 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR BAG

4

CHECK STARTER MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the ignition switch and that it is working properly

End of diagnosis

Fault in starter motor

Service the starter motor

Non-resettable fault in starter motor

Replace the starter motor

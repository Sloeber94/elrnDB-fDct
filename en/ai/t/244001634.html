Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle brake lights are located behind the side lights.

All the bulbs are operated each time the brake pedal is pressed via a switch on the actual pedal.

The circuit is supplied with the key ON and is protected by a specific fuse housed in the juntion unit under the instrument facia. A braking signal is also taken from the ABS and CRUISE CONTROL system.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The air bag warning light does not come on at start-up (it should come on for 3 seconds to indicate that the initial control unit test has been carried out and then go off)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The warning lights take the form of leds built into the electronic circuit and are NOT therefore replaceable

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the tester input.Check that the tester communicates properly with the air bag control unit and in particular:

  • for faults in the air bag control unit;
  • the plausibility of all the parameters

Move on to Step

1. No communication with the control unit

1. Restore:

  • connection between the air bag control unit and diagnostic connector;
  • air bag control unit supply

See E7030 AIRBAG

2. Error detected in the control unit

2. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

Move on to Step

1. Connections not correct

1. Repair the connections

2. Instrument panel not working

2. Replace instrument panel

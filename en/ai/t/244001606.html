Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is fitted with two special main beam headlamps.

These are activated:

  • with the dipped beams on, by pushing the stalk toward the facia (stable mode);
  • in flashing mode, by pulling the lever toward the steering wheel (unstable mode).

The circuit is protected by a special fuse and controlled by a relay - both located in the engine bay control unit.

A warning light on the instrument panel indicates main beam activation.

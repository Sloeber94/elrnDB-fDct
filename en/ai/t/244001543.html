Op. 7040H36 CENTRAL TRIM LOWER ELEMENT - R R

- Release the seat belt height adjustment knob.

- Remove the seat belt idler cover.

1. Undo the nut fixing the seat belt idler.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the seat belt reel.

3. Remove the seat belt.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

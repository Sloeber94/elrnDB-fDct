Drain the section of pipe from the repressurizing filler to the tank and the LPG supply and return pipes from the tank to the solenoid valve assembly / G4-G5, following the procedure and the instructions described below:

Op. 1043A02 LPG SYSTEM LINES PIPE DRAINING OPERATIONS

Return the vehicle to the workshop and place on a lift.

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1.870.833.000

LPG tank removal

1. Undo the bushes (1a) housing the guard fixing bolts and the fixing nuts (1b), keep the bushes and the nuts and remove the collars (1c).

Lower the hydraulic lift and remove the tank complete with supply and return pipes and electrical wiring.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

With the help of a second operator, remove the tank complete with supply and return pipes and electrical wiring; position the tank on suitable supports.

Once removed the tank should be left in the open in a safe placed shielded from bad weather.Place a label on the tank which has the following instructions: Do not touch - Do not overturn - No naked flames - No smoking - Do not use electrical equipment or heat lamps.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

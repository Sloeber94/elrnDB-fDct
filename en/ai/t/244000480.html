Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The interior lights do not work when the controls are operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

INDIVIDUAL BULB CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • the state and operation of the courtesy light switches;
  • Condition of the fuses;
  • the condition of the individual bulbs;
  • the condition of the bulb holder assemblies;

Move on to Step

1. Bulb or courtesy light faulty.

1. Repair or replace the courtesy light

2. Fuse/s blown.

2. Replace fuse/s and find out what could have caused them to blow.

3. Bulb or bulb holder faulty

3. Repair or replace the bulb holder

2

INTERIOR LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • switch control panel power supply
  • connections between switch panel and switches on doors
  • connections between courtesy lights and switch control unit
  • connections between courtesy lights and switches
  • courtesy light earth connection
  • switch earth connection

Move on to Step

1. Connections not correct

1. Restore the correct connections

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

2. Earth connection not correct

2. Restore earth connection

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

3. Power supply connection not correct

3. Restore power supply connection

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

3

DOOR SWITCHES CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check switch operation

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Switches faulty

Replace the switches

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

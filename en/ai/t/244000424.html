Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V, 2.8 JTD 8V; all production periods; only with equipment: 4X4

INTRODUCTION

when the four wheel drive is switched on the speed of the vehicle must NOT exceed 60 km/h.

the lockable rear differential (optional) should only be used if the road surface conditions provide little grip.

Use of the vehicle

Switching on the four wheel drive

the four wheel drive is only switched on when the LED in the special button is lit up The LED may light up or go out with a slight delay in relation to the command (the time required for the synchronization of the front and rear transmission).

Locking the rear differential (where fitted)

only lock/release the rear differential with the vehicle stationery and the four wheel drive on. The rear differential lock is only switched on when the LED in the special button is lit up.

only switch the rear differential lock on for journies on muddy road surfaces with poor grip conditions. Engage the gears without making sharp moves (especially when driving uphill). On leaving the muddy area, unlock the rear differential.

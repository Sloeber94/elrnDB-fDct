Removing

Refitting

See in Op. 1048A10

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel vapour pipe from the active charcoal filter.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

1. Remove the retaining band (1a) and release the pipe from the fuel vapour separator (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the pipe in the engine compartment.

- Fit the pipe on the petrol vapour separator and restore the retaining band removed previously.

- Secure the pipe using the retaining springs.

- Connect the rapid connector for the fuel vapour pipe to the active charcoal filter.

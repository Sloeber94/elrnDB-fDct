Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the water shield.

The operation of removing the water shield is destructive. To prevent damaging penetration, a new shield must be used when refitting.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt fixing the descending window glass guide.

2. . Remove the descending window glass guide.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Insert the guide in the special housing in the door frame.

- Secure the guide to the door frame tightening the fixing bolts.

- Clean the area where the water shield is fixed to the door frame in order to remove any residues and make sure that the shield sticks properly.

- Fasten a new water shield to the door frame.
